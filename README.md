# 250 Years of Risk in Fifteen Minutes

**Every breakthrough solved one risk and created the next.**

*What 250 years of industrial innovation can teach us about AI on the plant floor.*

Presented by **Grayson Estes** as a lightning talk at **CYBR.SEC.CON 2026**.

## The Talk

For 250 years, major technological breakthroughs have followed a remarkably
similar pattern: they solve an existing constraint while introducing an entirely
new class of risk.

Steam brought boiler explosions.

Electricity introduced shock and fire.

Assembly lines created systemic interdependence.

Connected control systems created cyber-physical attack paths.

AI introduces something different again: autonomous and cognitive risk.

This talk looks at that history and asks a simple question:

> What can 250 years of industrial innovation teach us about deploying AI
> safely on the plant floor?

## The Core Idea

The history of industrial technology suggests that we don't eliminate risk
through innovation — we trade one class of risk for another.

For industrial AI, the challenge isn't simply building a better model.

AI needs operational context:

- Assets
- Relationships
- History
- Process
- People

And none of those arrive simply by making the model bigger.
