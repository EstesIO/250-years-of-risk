250 Years of Risk in Fifteen Minutes

Every breakthrough solved one risk and created the next. What 250 years of industrial innovation can teach us about AI on the plant floor.
View the Presentation → Download PDF

The Idea

For 250 years, technological breakthroughs have followed a familiar pattern: they remove an existing constraint while introducing an entirely new class of risk.

What We Solved

Steam removed geographic constraints. Electricity transformed the architecture of work. Assembly lines radically increased throughput. Digital control replaced enormous amounts of mechanical complexity. AI can now reason across massive volumes of industrial information.

What We Introduced

Boiler explosions. Electrical shock and fire. System-wide interdependence. Cyber-physical attacks. And now autonomous systems capable of acting faster than humans can intervene.

We don't eliminate risk through innovation. We trade one class of risk for another.

AI Needs Operational Context

Industrial AI doesn't succeed simply because the model gets bigger. It needs context about assets, relationships, history, process, and people — the realities that determine how physical systems actually operate.

None of these arrive by making the model bigger.

About the Talk

Presented by Grayson Estes as a lightning talk at CYBR.SEC.CON 2026. The presentation explores 250 years of industrial innovation to ask what history can teach us about introducing AI into operational technology and cyber-physical environments.
